This module addresses the two main tax requirements in Thailand.

1. Tax Invoice (VAT)
--------------------

The point of tax invoice can occur either on the invoice (VAT) or on the
payment (undue VAT, which becomes VAT on payment).

- Tax invoices are recorded on journal entries and listed in Accounting
  > Accounting > Tax Invoices, the source of the Thai VAT report.
- The number and date of a purchase tax invoice are filled in from the
  vendor's tax invoice, sales tax invoices are numbered when posting.
- They replace the sales tax invoices of the standard ``l10n_th``
  module, which are not created, and its ``VAT Registered`` option is
  hidden.

2. Withholding Tax and Personal Income Tax
------------------------------------------

It is built on the standard ``l10n_th`` and
``l10n_account_withholding_tax`` modules, for Thai companies only. The
withholding tax is deducted during the payment, with two kinds of
calculation.

1. Fixed rate of the base amount, e.g., service 3%
2. Progressive rate of the yearly accumulated income, i.e., personal
   income tax (PIT)

Withholding tax certificates, PND forms and the withholding tax and PIT
reports are provided on top of it.

Configuration
=============

Withholding Tax
---------------

Withholding taxes are standard taxes, there is no specific master data.

- Go to Accounting > Configuration > Accounting > Taxes and create a tax
  with ``Is Withholding Tax`` checked and a negative rate, e.g., -3% for
  a service
- Set the withholding tax payable account on its tax repartition
- Set the standard ``Income Tax Type`` of the tax, it is the default
  type of income on the withholding tax certificate
- Optionally, set a PND tax tag on the repartition lines of the tax,
  e.g., ``PND53`` or ``Income PND53``, as the standard 50 Tawi report.
  It is the default PND form of the certificate, otherwise PND53 for
  companies and PND3 for individuals. Tags for PND3 and PND53 are
  standard, this module adds PND1, PND2 and PND3a.

Go to Accounting > Configuration > Settings and select the
``Withholding Tax Base`` account. It is used by the ``WH Base`` and
``Counterpart`` lines of the payments with withholding tax.

To print the payer signature on the 50 Tawi, go to Accounting >
Configuration > Settings > Thai Localization and check
``50 Tawi - Print Signature``. The name of the user who confirmed the
certificate is printed above the signature line.

The codes of the income types, used on the certificates, are available
in Accounting > Configuration > Accounting > WHT Income Code.

Personal Income Tax (PIT)
-------------------------

- Go to Accounting > Configuration > Accounting > Taxes and create the
  PIT tax as a withholding tax, then check ``Personal Income Tax``. The
  rate is fixed to 0% (the amount is hidden), the amount to withhold is
  computed on the payment. There can be only one PIT tax per company.
- Go to Accounting > Configuration > Accounting > PIT Rate to review the
  rate tables. There is one table per calendar year, the default rates
  in effect since 2017 are provided. Create a new table when the rates
  change.

Tax Invoices
------------

**Preparing Undue Tax**

- Use Developer Mode
- In Chart of Accounts, add a new account for Undue Tax, if it does not
  already exist.
- As admin, add the access right ``Show Full Accounting Features``
- Go to Accounting > Configuration > Settings and select ``Cash Basis``,
  this will open the cash basis options in the Tax master data
- Go to Accounting > Configuration > Accounting > Taxes, create new
  undue taxes for both sales and purchase

**Sales Tax Invoice Number**

The sales tax invoice is numbered when the entry is posted.

- For sales taxes (tax scope = Sales), choose the
  ``Tax Invoice Sequence`` (or create a new one) to number the tax
  invoice by sequence. Set the same sequence for the same tax on
  payment, i.e., Output VAT7% and Undue Output VAT7% must select the
  same sequence, so that both result in the same tax report.
- Otherwise, the tax invoice number is the number of the invoice. For
  undue VAT, go to Accounting > Configuration > Settings > Thai
  Localization and select the ``Customer Tax Invoice Number``, either
  the payment number or the invoice number.

Note: these options are not available for purchase taxes, because the
tax invoice number is from the vendor and filled in manually.

**Keep Zero Line Taxes**

To keep tax lines with zero amount (such as 0% VAT) in journal entries,
so that they appear in the tax invoices:

- Go to Accounting > Configuration > Settings and select
  ``Keep Zero Line Taxes``
- Create an invoice or bill and select a tax with 0% rate
- Go to Journal Items, a tax line with zero value is created

Zero withholding tax lines are never kept.

Usage
=====

Withholding Tax
---------------

**Vendor bills and customer invoices**

- Create the bill or invoice and set the withholding tax in ``Taxes`` of
  the lines. Only one withholding tax is allowed per line.
- Post it, then click Pay (Register Payment)
- In the payment wizard, select ``Withhold`` (pay and withhold, or
  withhold only), the withholding lines are proposed from the taxes of
  the bill. Their base and amount can be edited.
- Create the payment. The amounts withheld are recorded on the payment
  with the ``WH Base`` and ``Counterpart`` lines.

**Manual journal entries**

Set the withholding tax in ``Taxes`` of a journal item. The withholding
tax line is computed directly on the entry.

**Withholding tax certificates**

- On a posted payment or journal entry with withholding tax, an alert
  tells that the certificate is not yet created. Click
  ``Create withholding tax cert.``
- The certificates are created per partner and PND form. The PND form
  defaults from the PND tax tags of the tax, otherwise from the partner,
  i.e., company: PND53, individual: PND3, and can be edited. The type of
  income defaults from the income tax type of the tax, and the tax payer
  from the withholding condition of the payment. Check the type of
  income and income code, then confirm the certificate.
- Certificates are listed in Accounting > Vendors > WHT Certificates.
- Print a done certificate with ``Print 50 Tawi``, the standard 50 Tawi
  report of ``l10n_th`` filled from the certificate. Several
  certificates are downloaded as a ZIP file with Action > Thailand:
  Print 50 tawi. These replace the standard ones of payments, which are
  hidden.
- Send the 50 Tawi of a done certificate to the partner with
  ``Send by Email``.
- Resetting to draft or cancelling the payment or journal entry also
  cancels its certificates.

**Reports**

Go to Accounting > Reporting > Thai Accounting Reports > Withholding Tax

Personal Income Tax (PIT)
-------------------------

- Set the PIT tax in ``Taxes`` of the bill lines, e.g., for freelancers.
  It is 0% on the bill.
- Click Pay and select ``Withhold``. The PIT is computed on the payment
  from the yearly income accumulated by the partner, with the PIT rate
  table of the payment year. The yearly income includes the base of the
  posted payments and journal entries of the partner.
- A certificate can be created from the payment as for any other
  withholding tax.
- Go to Accounting > Reporting > Thai Accounting Reports > Personal
  Income Tax to see the yearly income and the PIT withheld.

Tax Invoice
-----------

The tax invoice is recorded on each journal entry with VAT, in the
``Tax Invoice`` tab, and listed in Accounting > Accounting > Tax
Invoices. The fields are the tax invoice number and date, and the number
of months of late report.

Note: the table ``account.move.tax.invoice`` is the main source of the
Thai VAT report.

**Customer Invoice and Payment**

- Create a customer invoice and choose the sales VAT or undue VAT
- Post the invoice and register the payment as usual. The tax invoice is
  numbered when posting, by the tax sequence, or by the invoice or
  payment number (see the configuration).

**Vendor Bill with Input VAT**

- Create a vendor bill and choose the Input VAT
- In the ``Tax Invoice`` tab, fill in ``Tax Invoice`` and ``Tax Date``
  given by your vendor. They are required before posting.
- Post the vendor bill, the tax invoice keeps the number and date.

**Vendor Bill with Undue Input VAT**

- Create a vendor bill and choose the Undue Input VAT, then post it
- Register the payment as usual
- The cash basis entry that clears the undue VAT is created, but it
  stays in **Draft** until the vendor's tax invoice is filled in
- In the ``Tax Invoice`` tab of the payment, fill in the Tax Invoice
  Number and Tax Date, then click ``Clear Tax``. The cash basis entry is
  **Posted** (or post the entry itself).

Note: when a bill is netted with a refund or a journal entry, Odoo 20
does not create a cash basis entry.

**Report Late**

A vendor tax invoice dated in an earlier month than the accounting date
can be reported late, up to 6 months. ``Report Late`` is proposed when
the tax invoice date is filled in, and the report date is then moved to
the end of the later month.

**Split**

In draft, use the ``Split`` button on a line of the ``Tax Invoice`` tab
to copy it, when the vendor issues several tax invoices for the same
journal item. Adjust the tax base and tax amount of each one.

**Credit Notes and Reversals**

When reversing a vendor bill or entry, the reversal wizard asks for the
vendor credit note number and date. The tax invoice of the reversal has
a negative base and amount.

**Manual Tax Invoice**

On journal entries, check ``Manual Tax Invoice`` on a journal item
(e.g., a VAT account line without tax) to create its tax invoice, for
example, for a purchase VAT recorded manually.

Changelog
=========

20.0.1.0.0
----------

**Breaking changes**

- Rebuilt on top of the standard ``l10n_th`` and
  ``l10n_account_withholding_tax`` modules. The withholding taxes are
  standard taxes (``Is Withholding Tax``). Tax invoices and withholding
  tax certificates apply to Thai companies only.
- Removed the models ``account.withholding.tax`` and
  ``account.withholding.move``, the ``WHT Account`` flag on accounts,
  the withholding tax on products and ``wht_tax_id`` on journal items.
- The standard Thai tax invoices of ``l10n_th`` (``VAT Registered``) are
  not created, the tax invoices of this module are used instead.
- Removed the ``Default Type of Income`` of taxes, the type of income of
  the certificates defaults from the standard ``Income Tax Type``.
- There is no data migration from older versions.
