Structured Thai addresses for contacts. The standard address fields stay
filled, so reports and integrations keep working.

- **Sub-districts and districts**: the 928 districts (khet/amphoe) are
  loaded as cities, and the 7,436 sub-districts (khwaeng/tambon) as a
  new model, with their DOPA code, Thai and English names and postal
  codes. Selecting a sub-district fills the district, province, zip and
  City, e.g. ``แขวงพระบรมมหาราชวัง เขตพระนคร``.
- **Address details**: house no., moo, village, building, floor, room
  no., soi, yaek and road have their own fields. Street and Street 2 are
  generated from them, in a configurable layout.

.. IMPORTANT::
   This is an alpha version, the data model and design can change at any time without warning.
   Only for development or testing purpose, do not use in production.
   `More details on development status <https://odoo-community.org/page/development-status>`_

Configuration
=============

The installation enables *Enforce Cities* on Thailand (*Contacts >
Configuration > Localization > Countries*). Disable it to type the City
instead of selecting the sub-district and district.

To change the layout of the street lines, activate the developer mode
and edit *Thai Street Format* on *Thailand*. Line 1 is Street, line 2 is
Street 2. Default:

::

   %(l10n_th_house_number)s %(l10n_th_moo)s %(l10n_th_village)s %(l10n_th_building)s %(l10n_th_floor)s %(l10n_th_room)s
   %(l10n_th_soi)s %(l10n_th_yaek)s %(l10n_th_road)s

Usage
=====

To use this module:

1. Open a contact of Thailand.
2. In *Address Detail*, select the *Sub-district*: the district,
   province, zip and City are filled.
3. Fill the details without their prefix, e.g. ``สุขุมวิท 101`` for the
   soi: Street and Street 2 are generated.

Notes:

- Editing Street or Street 2 directly keeps the typed text; editing a
  detail generates the street lines again.
- Typing another City, province or country removes the district and
  sub-district.
- Contacts share the sub-district and details of their company.
- The sub-districts are listed in *Contacts > Configuration >
  Localization > Sub-districts (TH)*.
